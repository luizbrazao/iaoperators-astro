Ah, Grok 3! The new toy that is making the rounds in the world of artificial intelligence, capturing looks, comments and the occasional "wow!" between experts and curious. The thing is simple: this model is not only playing in the major leagues, but, according to some, it is already beating them. If you're here, you probably want to know why everyone is talking about Grok 3, so let's break it down together.

We witness how Grok 3 not only promises to transform the AI ​​industry, but also manages to materialize these promises with practical cases and a technical deployment worthy of note. Here you have the summary with everything you need to know, but in the "Luiz" style, because no boring texts, let's bring it to life!

## A Model that Thinks and Creates (Almost like a genius)

Grok 3 is not the typical model that throws out random responses or leaves you with an “hmm... I don't know”. This young man has shown that he can handle complex tasks such as recreating the video game **"Portal"**. Yes, Portal, that classic we all love, with its portal guns and brain-melting puzzles. The trick? He executed essential game mechanics so smoothly that it makes you question whether you're talking to a model or a star developer.

## The Superhuman Infrastructure: Colossus Supercomputer

Of course, for something so powerful, you need a giant brain... or, rather, a supercomputer that takes your breath away. In this case, we are talking about **Colossus**, a titan that was put together in just 122 days (they spend more days fixing the Wi-Fi in my house, but oh well). They didn't bother building a new building to house it, they used existing infrastructure and **100 Nvidia H100 GPUs**, which, in simple terms, are the Ferrari of GPUs. Oh, and innovative water cooling, because of course, a titan like this needs to stay cool at all times.

## What Do Cracks Think of AI?

### Andre Karpathy, the critic everyone listens to

Basically, Andre Karpathy, who knows AI like no one else, made it very clear that Grok 3 is **"almost state of the art"**. He highlighted that, in specific tests, Grok competes head-to-head with models from giants such as OpenAI and DeepMind. Although, be careful, not everything is perfect and Karpathy mentioned that in certain areas the results are mixed. Of course, the price of Grok 3 can be much friendlier than that of its competitors and that, my people, is already a triumph in itself.

### Lex Fridman: the sensei shares his experience

Another with a seal of approval is Lex Fridman. This tech guru tested Grok 3 and came away impressed, especially with its possible future voice interaction. Imagine having a fluid conversation not with a person, but with a model who seems to understand everything. Lex is excited, and if he is, maybe it's time to listen to him.

## Comparisons, Simulations and More Experiments

For the more technical (or geeks, let's not kid ourselves), Grok 3 doesn't just process data or answer complex questions. In a simulation test using ASCII numbers and other “physics for geniuses” type visual challenges, it showed very high graphic ability. Compared to models like Claude 3.5 or Grok Mini (its younger brother), this model shines in several experiments. Of course, performance varies depending on the simulation, reminding us that no AI is perfect (yet!).

### What do other experts say?

Big names in the sector, such as Tom Gruber (co-founder of Siri) and Alexander Wang (CEO of Scale AI), also joined the praise train. They and other experts highlighted Grok 3's impressive learning speed and its focus on evolving in almost record time. This model seems to be ahead of the future while everyone else is watching the clock!
