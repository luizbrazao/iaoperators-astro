Have you realized that we are witnessing a true technological revolution? No, I'm not exaggerating. Artificial intelligence (AI) is no longer just the realm of science fiction movies; It is transforming how we work, create and, basically, live. And the best thing is that, today, you and I can access impressive tools that simplify tasks that once seemed titanic. Because? Because AI not only thinks, it also edits, automates and, basically, makes coffee for you (metaphorically speaking, of course).

Today we are going to immerse ourselves in a fascinating universe of AI tools: from extremely simple and free image editors, to models that generate entire websites or create interactive games. All without having to be an expert in programming or using more neurons than necessary. Shall we begin?

## What is this combination between Google and Gemini?

If you are one of those who think that Google is just your favorite search engine and Gmail, think again! Gemini is like the crown jewel in Google's artificial intelligence arsenal. And you know what? Now allied with a miraculous “no-code” automation tool. The most exciting thing is that you can automate entire processes without programming a single line of code. We're talking about creating applications capable of extracting text, generating video scripts, and even coordinating complex tasks as if you were a potential Netflix producer.

## China is killing it with new AI tools

In China, things are moving at full speed, and when I say "full speed," I mean that every week a revolutionary new tool seems to come out. Among these, **Grock** stands out, a wonder for editing images that is not only powerful, but completely free. But wait, have you ever heard of something as sophisticated as a function called "nanobanana"? Me neither, but for some reason I already want to use it. The trick? Grock allows you to edit images in a super fluid way, adding filters, changing backgrounds or even putting a Santa Claus hat on a red panda... because, why not?

## Playing with images: Pandas, filters and animations

Imagine this: you have a photo of an adorable red panda and, in less than a minute, you decide to not only edit it, but customize it to make it look as Christmassy as possible. Well, you upload your image to Grock, adjust a couple of presets, and bam, you have your masterpiece ready in minutes. But be careful, it doesn't just end there. You can also animate images, add music and voice to turn those photos into clips that could look like they came straight from Pixar, but without requiring any complex skills.

## Smart models: GLM 4.7 by Zai

### What makes this model different?

Now we move on to something that sounds very technical but promises amazing results. The GLM 4.7 model developed by Zai is like the "all-purpose" of language models. It is capable of generating web pages, 3D scenarios and much more in a matter of seconds. One of the most interesting examples we saw was how, with just a few instructions, this model created a functional website and an interactive Space Invaders-type game. The best thing is that you don't even need to be a genius to achieve it. At first glance, it seems that you are simply playing with words.

### And how about your results?

Obviously, not everything is perfect. Although it generates interesting structure and code, it is noticeable that there is still room for improvement when it comes to images or "really" contextualized content. But hey, we're exploring a tool that does in minutes what would take the average human days (or weeks).

## Tools that optimize the future

### Kiwin Image Edit from Alibaba

If you thought everything good had already been invented, wait until you see the latest version of Kiwin Image Edit, courtesy of Alibaba. Basically, this software is a multitasking editor that optimizes images with surgical precision. The best? You can run it both locally and online, depending on what you need. That is, you don't even need a super-powerful connection: you no longer have any excuses not to try it.

### And what does Google have to do with all this?

Ah, but let's go back to our beloved Google. In addition to Gemini, the company continues to tweak its usual tools and add new simple but powerful automation functions. Can you imagine writing a basic outline and having Google do all the heavy lifting, from data extraction to scripting? If having personal assistants is out of your budget, this will be your definitive option. I'm telling you: not even Marie Kondo organizes better.

## Conclusion: Is the future as accessible as it seems?

What really stands out about all these tools is the democratization of access to advanced technologies. Before, to do anything remotely similar, you needed either a huge wallet or a master's degree in computer engineering. But today, it doesn't matter if you're a graphic designer, a struggling entrepreneur, or someone exploring technological hobbies: you're one click away from transforming the ordinary into the extraordinary.

Do you dare to try these tools? Believe me, once you take the leap, there is no turning back. More than artificial intelligence, what we are seeing is pure magic available to anyone.
