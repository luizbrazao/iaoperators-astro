Have you wondered what the most in-demand jobs will be like in a few years? Surely yes, because we all ask ourselves that question when we see how technology advances by leaps and bounds. And here is the answer that perhaps you did not expect: in 2025, the big winners will not only be the traditional developers, designers or marketers. They will be the AI ​​generalists! So, if you've ever wanted to be like an all-rounder capable of doing a little bit of everything (and more), you're going to love this concept.

Liam Ottley, a visionary who has shined in the field of artificial intelligence earning a staggering four million dollars in just two years, shares this transformative perspective. According to him, mastering certain AI skills can be the springboard that many of us have been looking for to not only adapt to the future, but lead it. Do you want to know more? Let's dive into the world of AI generalists and their superpowers!

## The impact of AI on the labor market

First, let's put everything in context. According to studies by companies such as McKinsey and Bane, AI is making a strong impact on workplace dynamics. It is estimated that by 2030, up to 50% of the tasks we now perform could be automated. And although it sounds terrifying, let's not forget that each technological revolution has been accompanied by wonderful opportunities... if you know how to adapt.

This is where one of Darwin's most famous phrases comes into play: "It is not the strongest species that survives, nor the most intelligent, but the one that can best adapt to change." And now the dilemma: do you specialize more in your field or become an agile and versatile generalist? Spoiler alert: this article is clearly on the side of the generalists.

## Specialist or generalist? The debate explained

### Pandas and raccoons

To better understand this dichotomy, let's get into the animal world. Think of pandas as specialists: they eat basically one thing (bamboo) and are very good at it. But if bamboo suddenly disappeared... well, you can imagine. On the other hand, raccoons are generalists, eating almost whatever they find and adapting incredibly well to changing environments. Maybe you're already seeing the analogy, right?

Something similar is happening in our society. Specialized professions such as medicine, software development or even law face the growing threat of AI automation. So? Being a raccoon, a generalist capable of adding skills through artificial intelligence tools, seems to be the safest bet.

## What exactly does an AI generalist do?

An AI generalist not only understands how artificial intelligence tools work, but uses them to become a “jack of all trades.” They are people who acquire skills from multiple disciplines quickly and efficiently thanks to the power of AI.

A fascinating example is how the accumulation of skills can lead to extraordinary achievements. Let's mention here Sam Altman, CEO of OpenAI, who states that in the future it will not be unusual to see multi-million dollar companies created by a single person, thanks to artificial intelligence. These generalists are the modern Leonardo da Vinci: versatile, curious, and always ahead of the curve.

## The four superpowers of the AI ​​generalist

To stand out as an AI generalist in this exciting future, you'll need to master four major core skills:

  - **Power to Build:** Use AI tools to create software and applications without needing to be an expert programmer.

  - **Power to Automate:** Simplify repetitive processes and free up time by creating AI agents that work for you 24/7.

  - **Power to Create:** Generate professional quality content for any purpose, be it videos, music, text or graphics.

  - **Power of Connection:** Develop a personal brand and build an audience by leveraging AI technology to expand your reach.

### Power to build

In the past, creating software required endless hours of learning programming languages. Now, with tools like Bolt or Repet Agent, that is behind us. An example that demonstrates this power is that of a user who built a website in just five minutes using these tools. Five minutes! Never before have the barriers to entry for technological development been so low.

### Power to automate

Here lies a real gold mine: automation. Imagine having AI agents manage your emails, organize meetings, or even run business processes, all without you needing to constantly intervene. This type of automation not only improves productivity, but frees up your time to focus on higher-impact tasks.

### Power to create

Content is still king, and in an era dominated by social networks and digital businesses, knowing how to produce high-quality images, videos or music is an essential weapon. Thanks to AI tools, you can create what previously took days or weeks of work in a matter of minutes. But be careful, it's not just about generating content quickly, but about knowing how to edit and perfect it to make it stand out.

### Connection power

Last but not least is the talent for building relationships and communities. Being a generalist also means making meaningful connections, and AI tools can help you write better, understand your audience, and create a personal brand that makes an impact.

## Is the future yours?

In a world where adaptation is key, AI generalists have everything to win. And we're not talking about becoming the next genius of Silicon Valley, but about using these tools to live better, work smarter and take advantage of the opportunities that are at our fingertips.

Ready to join this revolution? You just need curiosity, desire to learn and the right tools. Because, if we have learned anything so far, it is that in times of change it is not the strongest that survives, but the one that best adapts. So, go ahead! Turn off Netflix for a while and start building that bright future that artificial intelligence is giving you.
