We are going to talk about something that sounds futuristic, but is already part of our present: intelligent assistants with generative AI. If you are part of the "I hate wasting time with repetitive tasks" team, this is going to interest you a lot. Because these guys don't just come to give you a hand... they come to literally revolutionize how you work. So sit back while I break down what you need to know about these tools, which are the top options on the market and even which one is best for you depending on the type of company you have. Let's start strong.

Can you imagine opening your email and having an assistant already read, analyzed and prepared a professional response for you? Or that you need a report and it is ready in two clicks? Well, this is no longer science fiction, friends, thanks to Microsoft Dynamics 365 Copilot, SAP Joule and Sage Copilot. Each one has their unique vibe, and we are going to see it.

## What are intelligent assistants with generative AI?

In short, they are tools designed to connect with companies' management systems (the famous ERP and CRM, those that some of us love and others fear). And what exactly do they do? Well, they turbocharge your daily tasks using generative AI. This means that they learn, process natural language (yes, you can talk to them as if you were a frustrated voice assistant yourself) and help both in specific areas and in strategic decisions.

### Where do they come into action?

  - **Sales:** Preparation of reports, personalized customer service, and forecasting results.

  - **Customer service:** They respond to emails, conversation summaries and quick queries.

  - **Marketing:** Data-based content creation and optimization of digital strategies.

  - **Operations:** Inventory management, financial reports... and any similar headache.

## The best of the best: Analyzing the big three

Now, it's time to compare familiar faces: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Copilot, SAP Joule and Sage Copilot. I'll tell you their strengths and where they shine most brightly.

### 1. Microsoft [Dynamics 365](https://www.microsoft.com/es-es/dynamics-365) Copilot

This co-pilot is the typical one you find in a super well-geared technological ecosystem. Being integrated with Microsoft (everything that sounds like Office 365), it is like bread and Nutella for any team that already uses Word, Excel and more.

  - **Collaboration with OpenAI:** That's nothing. It goes directly to increasing the productivity of teams with next-generation AI.

- **Pro level automation:** From answering frequently asked questions to updating account information without you having to lift a finger.

  - **Customer service:** Automatic email summaries, generation of quick responses, and all this in record time.

### 2. SAP Joule

Is your company already married to SAP? If so, [Joule](https://www.sap.com/spain/products/artificial-intelligence/ai-assistant.html) will be your new best friend. This wizard operates directly from the cloud and helps you extract gold (or useful data, rather) from already integrated systems.

  - **Operational optimization:** Get information in seconds about inventories, expenses and sales reports.

  - **Financial analysis:** Trace accounting operations as if you were Sherlock Holmes, but without glasses and with coffee in hand.

### 3. Sage Copilot

Ah, [Sage](https://www.sage.com/es-es/sage-copilot/). The co-pilot friend of small and medium-sized companies. Don't underestimate its versatility because it is specifically designed to make things simpler for accounting professionals and small teams.

  - **Maximum proactivity:** Manage cash flow, accelerate payments and even organize your tasks.

  - **Smart communication:** Generate personalized reports without your head exploding.

## Which one to choose according to your company?

Now that you know how these co-pilots play, let's make it even clearer for you. Here is a breakdown according to the size of your company:

      **Company Size**
      **Recommendation**

      Small Businesses
      **Sage Copilot:** Light, economical and specialized in finance.

      Medium Companies
      **Microsoft Dynamics Copilot:** Versatile and ideal for teams of a certain size.

      Large Companies
      **SAP Joule:** Perfect for large volumes of data and complex operations.
