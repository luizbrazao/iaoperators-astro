Are you tired of waiting ages while your AI assistant thinks, drinks coffee, meditates, and then gives you an answer? Well, let me introduce you to **Mistral**, the new player in the world of artificial intelligence, which not only promises speed, but also comes strong with a few quite striking abilities. And yes, I know that in a world full of *""GPTs""* you have to stand out to be taken seriously, and it seems that Mistral has done its homework.

In this post I'll take you by the hand to discover what makes this turbocharged chatbot so special, from its dizzying speed to its ability to create functional applications before you finish your coffee. Let's go there, because this is getting interesting.

## The speed of Mistral: the Usain Bolt of chatbots

If there is something that Mistral wants us to know from the beginning, it is that its speed is not a simple whim. It is your *core business*. We are talking about **1,000 tokens per second**. For those who don't speak the language of word processing, this means that this bot thinks and responds ten times faster than ChatGPT (or at least it presumes so in the direct comparison).

How is this speed noticeable? Imagine you are working on an app or looking to debug code. The speed of iteration that Mistral offers could save you minutes, hours, or even your entire lunch when you're in a pinch. Less “*wait a minute*” and more “*ready, here you go*”.

## An all-terrain intelligence?

Not only has Mistral mastered the art of responding quickly, but it also has an arsenal of built-in tools that make your proposal much more interesting. Let's take a closer look:

### Built-in features that surprise

  - **Canvas**: Ideal for creating interactive visualizations. Perfect if you want to impress your boss or colleague with real-time graphics.

  - **Code Runner**: Yes, you can write code directly, test and debug it without leaving the interface. For busy developers, this is like music to the ears.

  - **Generation of interactive images and documents**: Importing PDFs or generating images instantly is another of those features that you say: "ok, I didn't know I needed it, but now I can't live without it."

## Practical examples that excite

As a good AI, it wouldn't be complete without demonstrating what it can do in the real world. In the video that inspired this entry, two fascinating examples were developed:

### 1. Creating a mortgage calculator at the speed of light

Someone asked Mistral to create an application to calculate mortgage payments, and in a matter of seconds it was up and running. As if it were magic. The most interesting thing? It generated a functional interface that not only did calculations, but was ready to use. Goodbye confusing spreadsheets!

### 2. Transforming images to Halftone patterns

Then came the creative challenge. Create an application that could convert images to halftone patterns (those *comic pop art* type artistic designs). Mistral not only created a functional tool quickly, but also allowed customizing options such as buttons and sliders to adjust the final output.

The final conclusion was that, although impressive results were achieved, there was room to adjust it at the interface level, demonstrating that although fast, sometimes a couple of corrections are necessary. Come on, like any genius who needs to polish the edges.

## Speed ​​vs. Quality: What matters most?

Here comes the eternal dilemma: do you really need that much speed or do you prefer something more slow and perfected? The truth is that it depends on the context. If you're working in software development, the ability to iterate faster will inevitably improve your workflow. However, in tasks such as writing or creative generation, speed may not be the most relevant factor.

But Mistral is betting that speed can make a difference, especially in competitive environments where meeting tight deadlines is the norm.
