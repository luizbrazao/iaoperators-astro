## What is Goku and how does it work?

Goku is literally a next-generation video generator. It sounds technical, but let's translate it: you can write a text or upload an image, and the system converts this information into an epic quality video. All this with open source capabilities, which means that anyone can download it, modify it, or use it for both personal and business projects. It couldn't be more accessible. No paying monthly subscriptions or anything like that!

## How can you use it?

### 1. Download it from Github

Para los más tech-savvy, Goku está disponible para su descarga en [Github](https://saiyan-world.github.io/goku/) . Esto significa que puedes instalarlo en tu ordenador y manejarlo localmente. Perfecto si eres fan de tener control total sobre cada pixel que produces.

### 2. Use its online version

Si prefieres la simplicidad, puedes acceder a Goku a través de plataformas como [Replicate](https://replicate.com/tencent/hunyuan-video) o [Fal.ai](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/hunyuan-video). Aquí solo necesitas meter tus prompts (es decir, tus instrucciones), ajustar parámetros como tamaño o velocidad (FPS, para los más técnicos), ¡y voilà! Tu nuevo vídeo está servido.

Here comes the good part: the applications. Because, come on, what good is a tool if we don't know how to use it in real life. Goku has a lot of hidden talents and he will surprise you with these use cases:

  - **Product demos:** Imagine recording how your product works without having to hire models or rent studios. From a jacket to tech accessories, anything can be shown in action.

  - **Avatar Creation:** Do you need a custom avatar for your brand? Goku can generate dynamic virtual characters with just a textual description.

  - **Transform static images:** Your photos can come to life. Perfect for product presentations with a more professional touch.

  - **Fashion and styling:** Do you have a clothing brand? Goku can show you what your designs look like without having to host a shoot with real models. Cheap and practical.

  - **Marketing campaigns:** Do you want to launch promotional videos focused on different audiences? With Goku you can customize yourself to your liking and pleasure.

  - **Restaurants and food:** Oh, I love this one. Think about generating videos of your best live, hot and delicious dishes using only descriptions. Don't tell me your mouth isn't watering.

  - **Texts converted into video:** You can even transform long descriptions into dynamic animated videos. Perfect for blogs or dense content that needs more "life".
