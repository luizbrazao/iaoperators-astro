## So what is ARCHITECHTURES and what does it promise?

ARCHITECHTURES is not just a “pretty” program; It is an *artificial intelligence* capable of designing in real time. What does this mean? That you will no longer have to spend weeks adjusting repetitive details between shots. This technological marvel is designed especially for multi-construction residential buildings, automating processes that previously made us sweat (and cry).

But be careful, it's not just fast. ARCHITECHTURES promises surgical precision thanks to the implementation of project-specific data (such as local regulations). You enter parameters such as the number of floors, the distribution of departments and even regional regulations. The result? An optimized design ready to transform your screen into an incredible three-dimensional work environment.

## Let's try together:

### How the magic begins

In one of the examples presented, they create a project called “Wilson Building” located in Córdoba, Argentina (yes, they already got me hooked on the fancy name). First, they set up the metric system and begin loading the base data, such as CAD layers that establish the environment. It's like putting together a puzzle, but much more exciting!

An impressive detail is the real-time interactivity. Whether you need to adjust the number of apartments or play with the typology of the floors, everything responds in seconds. I dare say that the only thing faster would be to order a well-prepared pizza.

### Custom buildings… literally!

And speaking of adjustments, ARCHITECHTURES allows you to make changes individually per floor or simultaneously for the entire building. This includes modifications such as layout of one, two or three-bedroom apartments, optimization to comply with local regulations and, of course, the ability to detect (and solve) structural problems instantly with visual alerts. All this functionality makes sitting in front of the computer feel more like conducting an orchestra than working.

## Innovations that will make you say “why didn't we have this before?”

Has our modern architecture always been so complex? Well, it turns out that a large part of our time is consumed solving repetitive technical aspects. This is where ARCHITECHTURES shines:

  - **Smart Presets:** From regulations to costs and design objectives. Everything pre-configured so you don't have to start from scratch.

  - **Integrated topography:** Adds data from OpenStreetMap to adapt projects to terrain that would usually make us waste several hours or even days.

  - **Adapted design:** Uneven terrain? No problem. ARCHITECHTURES adjusts every corner and curve to respect the natural topography.

These features, while they sound cold and technological, actually give the architect back the power to focus on what matters: human interaction, creativity, and the art of building spaces that improve lives.
